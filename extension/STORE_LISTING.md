# Chrome Web Store listing

## Name
Business Review Reply AI

## Summary
Generate professional AI replies for Google Business Profile customer reviews in seconds.

## Detailed description
Business Review Reply AI helps business owners and local teams respond to customer reviews faster.

Open a review in Google Maps or Google Business Profile, generate a personalized response, edit it, and insert it into the reply field. Nothing is published automatically: you remain in control and review every response before posting.

Features:
- Detects the visible customer review
- Generates a response adapted to the rating and comment
- Lets you edit or copy the suggestion
- Inserts the response into the visible reply field
- Uses minimal Chrome permissions

Business Review Reply AI is an independent product and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google LLC.

## Category
Productivity

## Privacy practices
Single purpose: help users draft replies to customer reviews. Review text, star rating, reviewer display name, and the business name are sent securely to GoogleReviewAI's generation service only when the user clicks Generate. The extension does not sell user data, run advertising, or publish replies automatically.
