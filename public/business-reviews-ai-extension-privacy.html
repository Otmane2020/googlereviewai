Business Reviews AI

Chrome Extension Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

Business Reviews AI has one purpose: help a user draft a reply to a customer review the user is currently viewing, then let the user copy or insert that draft for review before publishing it themselves.

Data processed by the extension

When you explicitly open the extension and ask it to detect or generate a reply, it may process the visible review text, the review rating, and the public business name shown on the current page. The extension does not continuously monitor pages and does not run a background content script.

How data is used

The review text, rating, and business name are transmitted to the GoogleReviewAI backend hosted on Supabase only to generate the AI-assisted reply you request. The generated reply is returned to the extension so you can edit, copy, or insert it.

Local storage

The extension may save the business name locally in Chrome storage to avoid asking you to enter it repeatedly. It does not use this local value for advertising or cross-site tracking.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not sell user data.
  • It does not use review data for advertising or ad targeting.
  • It does not collect browsing history or continuously track browsing activity.
  • It does not automatically publish replies. The user remains in control of the final Google publish/reply action.
  • It does not collect passwords, payment details, health information, or precise location.

Third-party infrastructure

The AI generation request is processed through the GoogleReviewAI service infrastructure, including Supabase and the AI providers configured by the service. These services are used only to provide the requested reply-generation functionality.

Security and data rights

Requests are transmitted over HTTPS. For privacy questions, access, deletion, or other data-rights requests, contact privacy@googlereviewai.com.
